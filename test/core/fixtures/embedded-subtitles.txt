embedded-subtitles.mkv is 2 seconds of 32x32 black video starting at 1 second,
with the same three cues as a SubRip track (language eng) and as an ASS track
(language ger). No third-party media is included.

1  0.0 - 0.5  "Before video"    ends before the video starts
2  0.8 - 1.5  "Crossing start"  crosses the video start
3  2.0 - 2.5  "After start"     inside the video

Generated with FFmpeg 9.0 from lavfi color and the cues as cues.srt:
  ffmpeg -i cues.srt cues.ass
  ffmpeg -itsoffset 1 -f lavfi -t 2 -i color=black:size=32x32:rate=5 \
    -i cues.srt -i cues.ass -map 0:v -map 1:s -map 2:s \
    -c:v mpeg4 -c:s:0 srt -c:s:1 ass \
    -metadata:s:s:0 language=eng -metadata:s:s:1 language=ger \
    embedded-subtitles.mkv
