Gerbera is the name of this optional flower-based icon theme.

All 20 images in this theme were created on 1 and 2 November
2026 using OpenAI built-in image generation at the contributor's request.
The contributor makes the supplied artwork available under GPL-2.0-only,
on the same terms as Gerbera, with no additional restrictions.

Design provenance: the Gerbera flower/petal identity was derived from
the existing project artwork ../../logo-orange.svg. The original icon
series in the parent directory, particularly albums.png and tracks.png,
provided the dimensional material and lighting references. The approved
3D media-icon concepts informed the foreground materials and geometry.
Each icon uses multiple Gerbera flowers of different sizes and viewing angles,
with sculpted petals, shadows and distinct color families. High-contrast
foreground symbols remain prominent. The Albums collection and single Album
use distinct artwork. Recently Played uses a clock and play symbol; Recently
Modified uses a hammer and media-library symbol. The supplied Video PNG
includes the approved geometric correction to its play triangle.

These are generated raster interpretations of the Gerbera flower identity,
not exact vector-logo exports or extracted third-party icon-library assets.
The PNG files are the supplied artwork sources; no SVG, 3D mesh or layered
source is available.

The separate 20-icon Techno theme remains in the parent directory with its
own provenance and license notice. Its original 15 images are unchanged.
